[ FAQ ]

Questions

My package didn’t activate

Check that you bought it while logged into Whop with the Discord account connected to the Encore server. Step-by-step guide: connect Discord on Whop. If it still hasn’t activated, open a ticket on our Discord.

Can I combine packages?

Yes. All packages stack automatically — buy a second package and it is added on top of your current amount.

How do I reduce a package?

Cancel the package you want to reduce in Manage membership on Whop, then buy the size that fits you.

Who processes the payment?

Checkout and payments are handled by Whop. Billing, renewals and cancellations are managed in your Whop account.
